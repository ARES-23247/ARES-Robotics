# Third-party software

ARESLib is licensed under Apache-2.0, but its source and binary distributions use third-party software under separate licenses. Those licenses continue to apply to their respective components.

Major components include Kotlin and kotlinx libraries (Apache-2.0), WPILib (BSD-3-Clause), the FIRST Tech Challenge SDK (BSD-3-Clause-Clear), dyn4j (BSD-3-Clause), NanoHTTPD (BSD-3-Clause), Java-WebSocket (MIT), MessagePack (Apache-2.0), and Gson (Apache-2.0).

This summary does not replace the license texts and notices shipped by those projects. A binary redistributor must retain all notices included in dependency artifacts and in any bundled native-runtime archives.
