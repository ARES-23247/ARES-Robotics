# Contributing

ARES Analytics is publicly distributed under `AGPL-3.0-or-later` and may also be offered under separate commercial terms. Maintaining that dual-licensing option requires the project to hold sufficient relicensing rights for every accepted contribution.

External contributions may be merged only after the maintainers confirm that the contributor has accepted the project's contributor agreement. A Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off by itself does not grant the additional commercial-relicensing rights required here.

Do not submit third-party code without identifying its provenance and license, and do not remove existing copyright, license, or attribution notices. Until a reviewed contributor agreement and signing workflow are published, maintainers should not merge external copyrighted contributions into this repository.

New ARES-authored source files should identify their license with the language-appropriate SPDX header, for example:

```kotlin
// SPDX-License-Identifier: AGPL-3.0-or-later
```
